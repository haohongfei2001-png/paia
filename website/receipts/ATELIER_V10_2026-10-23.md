# Atelier V10 — presentation-led homepage

Owner request on 23 October 2026: implement the agreed visual-narrative review.
The existing site looked ordinary, rigid and overly dependent on visitors
operating mock interfaces. Preserve its approved highlights while improving the
Chinese type, art direction, proportions, archive, thoughts, reuse and daily value.

Deployment remains existing GitHub Pages, main/root, inputarchive.com.
No existing worktree, extension, credentials, services, runtime permissions,
installation availability, contact consent or workflow is modified.

## Delivered design

- Chinese display type is a locally hosted licensed light serif subset, with
  its own scale/line-height/tracking. The overly large, tightly tracked title
  is replaced with a smaller, optically spaced composition and open ribbon field.
- Archive: a remembered keyword reveals a readable expression through a finite
  visual sequence. No source panel or replica search-result application.
- Thoughts: the same three expression nodes move into a single theme, keeping
  their wording. The final composition uses a designed quotation-mark sculpture
  and a reading plane, not a fake topic creation or invented AI result.
- Prompt reuse: exact R3 component opens/collapses within a carefully cropped
  scene, writes only to the synthetic readonly draft, and does not send.
- Editing: a short typographic revision, not another editable form on the home.
- Daily value: one concrete saved instruction appears in a new sample draft;
  visitors do not have to choose a use-case tab to understand the benefit.
- The approved color-outline phrase, Context markup, privacy artwork and closing
  section were compared against the prior templates in both languages and are
  identical after excluding the old reveal class. Context permissions retain
  their exact independent/default-off behavior.

## Motion and factual limits

Each scene runs only once when visible. Pausing holds its phase and transitions;
leaving the viewport or hiding the page suspends it. Reduced motion/no JavaScript
exposes final readable compositions. Visitor control of the R3 component pauses
automatic state changes. All content is fictional; no real archive is fetched,
no model is called, no message is sent and no data is persisted.

Main merge, deployment and exact public-byte/browser readback are separate gates;
record their actual PR/Actions outcomes rather than inferring them from local tests.
This is website evidence, not installed-extension, live-AI or physical-device
acceptance and not a claim of subjective design approval.
