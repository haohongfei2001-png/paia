What happens when you close a topic?

That topic leaves the allowed scope. Items saved independently in other cards keep their own controls. Revoking access stops future reads; it cannot recall content already delivered to an external AI.

MY INFORMATION / MY RULES / MY NOW / MY INPUTS

Closed topics stay closed. Access does not fall back to your entire Input Archive.
