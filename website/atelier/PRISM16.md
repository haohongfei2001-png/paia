# PAIA / Prism 16 — optical presentation

Owner request: retain current copy and the recognized hero/Context/story assets,
but stop the small-paper/bulletin-board direction. Use a large perspective
Thought Library, precise thin lines, clear optical materials and actual
scroll-linked depth. Modify the official website rather than another study.

## Exact starting point and scope

Baseline main: `6d5470a71cfb8d7be692a2a6744a19d0f7086f1e` (V15 cache-entry repair).
This is not a rollback to the V13 or detached study. Work is in an isolated
website worktree. The supported product contract and actual content remain;
no extension, service, account, permission, contact, installation or CI workflow
is changed. The interactive demo and all interior pages remain byte-identical.

## Visual changes

- Input Archive is one precision surface, not a group of independently colored
  scraps. Its four original expressions keep exact wording and logical IDs.
  The primary expression is prominent; secondary content is held by quiet rules.
- Thought Library is an accessible, static-HTML optical object. The old runtime
  Shadow DOM replacement is retired for this release, not layered on top.
  Exact three topics/nine chapters and the two complete original inputs remain.
- Separate rear plate, rim, topic rail and reading plane have genuine CSS 3D
  positions. Native scrolling moves a bounded camera from an oblique view to a
  clear face-on result. No wheel capture or forced scroll, and no time-driven
  replacement of the author's words. A pause control produces a static object.
- Thin original contour artwork is a mathematically generated folded surface,
  not a borrowed competitor asset, logo or illustration. Blur is restricted to
  translucent chrome; the reading surface remains effectively opaque.
- Reuse and editing cards use the same clear surfaces and edges rather than
  tilted paper fragments. Original R3 implementation, glyph and six-row board
  are preserved exactly. The recognized privacy orbit remains.
- Copy is not rewritten. Hero, manifesto, Context, story and CTA fragments are
  byte-locked; Archive/Thought paragraphs and the detail text are text-locked.
- Narrow layouts use a stationary full-width reader with compact topic directory;
  reduced-motion preference removes the extra scroll distance and perspective.

## Ownership and verification

Canonical `home.zh.html` / `home.en.html` still go through `website/build.py`.
`prism16.css` is homepage-scoped. `presentation16.js` retains the established
menu/R3 and finite reuse owner and adds a passive, event-driven native-scroll
camera. All assets are same-origin; no remote font or model/data call is added.
Old assets remain immutable for earlier references, but this homepage loads only
the new immutable entry. No shadow fetch/failure race can replace the new scene.

`preserved-prism16.json` locks 149 prior deployed assets/non-home outputs and
approved fragments to the exact starting commit. `prism16_checks.py` verifies
the new visible DOM, full prose, layout, actual scroll matrices, static/reduced
alternatives, keyboard navigation, default-off independent permissions and
original R3 behavior. It supplements the unchanged demo/IME/caret-insertion,
contact consent, release and policy tests; prior-version checks are retained.

Run the existing commands `python website/build.py --check`,
`python website/test.py`, and `python website/verify_live.py` after Pages.
Browser screenshots and scroll samples are reviewed separately from assertions.
These are website checks, not user research, installed-extension acceptance,
physical-device measurements or proof of aesthetic quality.
