# Continuity v2 — scoped design decision

The owner rejected the moving-home case and requested the preceding critique's
improvements. The baseline is production 1f3c826, not an earlier rejected draft.

The first example is a freelancer quoting-tool project developed across six
fictional conversations from March to September. It includes unresolved demand,
rejected scope, changed interaction and a next test. Research and career cases
are equally concrete alternatives, not testimonials or real private records.
No family recipes, moving-house decisions, podcast/writing exercises, invented
adoption figures or emotional autobiography are introduced.

Palette: paper #ffffff, surface #f4f7fc, ink #202c3c, secondary #52647b,
blue #466cae, line #dce4ef. Keep the existing brand face and system UI text.
English display may use the existing Instrument Serif for the one opening
statement; Chinese stays a deliberately spaced sans. Body words remain at least
16px at ordinary scale; sources are subordinate without shrinking below 11px.

The characteristic moment is the same original words seen by conversation and
by topic, followed by an old decision visibly carried into a new request. It is
not a general-purpose app screenshot or a third-party hero illustration.

Layout:
    retained translucent navigation
    quiet statement | a working before/after of the same words
    real sample search + original source
    broad continuous reader / three substantive case choices
    compact prompt companion + a draft with an explicit quote action
    retained four-card Context / compact local-first note
    retained original closing and creator's drawing

No pretend search field, dead three-space tabs, decorative ellipsis action or
fake model processing. Presentation switches are ordinary buttons; sources use
native disclosures. The default reading outcome is visible, no scroll gate.
Motion explains the switch, respects reduced motion and never runs at rest.

Retain source/working distinction, draft+selection/IME protection, independent
Context permission owners and the real install manifest. No product or service
implementation changes. Full original demo behavior remains under its existing
regressions; synthetic titles/text/date assertions are explicitly updated.
