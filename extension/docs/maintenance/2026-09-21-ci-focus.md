## Diagnosis and minimal correction

A real headless-browser MutationObserver captured the Original pane becoming
visible with `{focused:true, disabled:true, checked:false}` on the AI toggle.
Disabling the control can retain its old focus briefly before native blur; the
normal switch finalizer enables and refocuses it after asynchronous reading.
The old focus-only predicate therefore could succeed before switch completion,
then its following assertion could land in the normal blur interval. Recorded
focus events showed disable at3014.2ms, blur3020.4ms and final enable/focus3037.5ms
in one diagnostic run. These timings explain a race, not a required delay.

The test now waits atomically for Original selected, toggle enabled and focus on
that same toggle. The original exact focus assertion and subsequent keyboard
Space back to cached AI content remain unchanged. No delay/retry/timeout was added
to the test and no product focus behavior was changed.

On narrow screens the current Reader intentionally hides primary navigation.
After reload, the old helper could read pre-restoration aria-current and attempt
another navigation while the original route restoration made that target hidden.
The test now waits for the original Topic heading and verifies its actual history
Topic identity after reload. It does not navigate again or force-click hidden UI.
Initial user navigation and all candidate, IME, stale-CAS, provider-failure,
worker-interruption and no-hidden-request assertions remain.
