# Settings Consumer v2 — simplified final presentation

**SET2-UI-1.2 / OWNER_APPROVED / 2026-10-13.** The owner accepted removal of redundant Settings explanation/sample presentation and the elaborate first-use experience. [Shared UX §§3/5/6](UX_CONTRACT.md) and [PLAN](SETTINGS_CONSUMER_V2_PLAN.md) specify the final target. Keep the blue-white visual family, actual functional inventory and existing implementation owners; no new Settings plan or product lane.

## 1. Settings composition: useful controls, no demonstration area

Keep the lightweight six-group directory, flat settings rows, subtle blue-white surfaces, title/control/focus roles and current narrow/dark composition. Ordinary rows show a clear label and current value/control with only necessary state. Remove repeated page/group slogans, decorative helper paragraphs and explanations that merely repeat a clear label. Collapse the freed vertical space without shrinking effective touch targets or crowding wrapped labels.

**Remove the entire reading-preview sample panel**, example text/time, live typography demonstration and its reserved height. Do not retain a smaller version, move it to a drawer or add a preview toggle. User selects named font/width/time values, receives honest persistence feedback and sees the effect in actual reading pages. The approved shared prose rhythm is UX §3; no extra line-spacing control is added.

The removal of explanation is not removal of necessary decision information. Keep concise, action-local capture/filter distinctions, real error/unknown/pending feedback and actual risk/consent/restore/account-transfer/deletion scope. Do not scatter a question-mark control on every setting as a replacement. Help and substantive source/legal detail remain available through their existing destinations, not mandatory teaching on every page.

## 3. Auxiliary pages and first use

### Minimal first-use permission, not a welcome page

Cancel the dedicated welcome/marketing page, large brand promise, two-column example document, sample quote, 先看看示例 branch and mandatory intro/consent/import walkthrough. Reach the actual app immediately; do not replace neutral Archive Main with a smaller welcome poster.

Use the existing capture owner for one compact consent surface only when consent is genuinely undecided: clear heading, short accurate scope statement, 开始保存 / 暂不开始, and one collapsed/link entry for full boundaries. It must identify saving sent/displayed user text, not disguise collection behind an unlabeled start action. Keep the full actual exclusions and pause/resume/time disclosures available. Do not impose a second mandatory tutorial/confirmation solely to copy the old prototype. Preserve any truly required platform consent.

Decline/dismiss keeps capture off and allows continued app use; honor the existing dismissal/decision lifetime rather than block every reopen. Settings remains the later explicit enable entry. Enable only after acknowledged consent/command success; failed persistence remains off with concise retry. Do not seed sample content or implicitly enable imports, suggestions, cloud, AI access or billing. Existing ongoing users do not get a new first-use tour.

Source/history and object-specific purge stay contextual; no Settings history/purge product is revived.

Use short confirmation for an actual decision, temporary source inspection drawer, and normal page for long documents/substantive workflows. Preserve useful import preview, human-conflict comparison and destructive impact review: **removing the typography example does not remove these real user-decision previews**. No explanatory layer is added merely for appearance. Existing About/help/verified legal/contact content and safe feedback draft/copy/send boundaries remain.
