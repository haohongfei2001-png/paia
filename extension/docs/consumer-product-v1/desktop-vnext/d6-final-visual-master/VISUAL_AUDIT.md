## Root cause, not blame-shifting

The previous handoff converted parts of the approved image into abstract tokens and independently tested component crops. That can produce correct behavior without a satisfactory complete page. D6 therefore fixes finished whole-window visual targets first. It does not claim every D5 improvement was wrong, that the owner failed to update, or that green CI proves final visual quality.
