## User-visible outcome

When capture disconnects or a page is stale, PAIA explains the interruption and gives one safe action before further capture. Storage pressure, failed local save/read, unavailable search/AI and update-check failure each have bounded copy and recovery controls. An unsuccessful write, index, AI call or update is never presented as success. Previously these failures exposed inconsistent low-level states or left the next action unclear. Existing saved archive content remains readable; the UI does not automatically refresh a page with unsent text or retry a paid AI call.
