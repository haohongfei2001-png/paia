## Remaining engineering

This is actual cold recovery at the tested small scale. Revalidation and prefix closure are fully charged against the existing 16 MiB data-work task and physical 4096-reference / 2 MiB / 30-second limits. They do not refresh those limits after restart. The earlier 65/129 capacity refusals remain unresolved.

A future large-graph publisher needs a genuinely frozen generation across bounded verification pages, exact writer-version linkage, durable page coverage and a small final publication transaction. The present fixed seal still rereads all physical expected rows. Simply deleting that last read or treating a cached graph as current durable truth would weaken the demonstrated late-mutation protection and is not implemented here. The repository gate is not a sandbox against arbitrary direct native IndexedDB access; any new paged design must state and preserve its precise writer boundary and prove no permitted original writer can alter a certified page without invalidation.

Evidence is under `work/canonical-generation-human-cold-20261023/`, including separate unsuccessful runs, final test logs, source vector and SHA256 manifest. Body-free control transaction diagnostics remain separately reported from prepaid data work, as documented by the preceding semantic-closure correction.
