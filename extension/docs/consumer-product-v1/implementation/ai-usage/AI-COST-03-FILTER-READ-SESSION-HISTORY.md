## Bounds and lifecycle

- Read and financial currentness share eight transport slots, including unresolved fetch/proof work. Cancel does not free an unsettled slot.
- Read-session owners sharing the source hub share eight qualifications, eight pages, 200 distinct pinned operations and 48 MiB charged retained history data. The production source assembly owns that single hub and drains a retiring hub before replacement; arbitrary independently constructed hubs are not claimed to share these page limits. Two actual Stores sharing one hub cannot reset the allowance. The two private retained data copies are charged. History storage has a separate eight-task allowance keyed by original repository. These are separate from transient bounded original-owner reads and the terminal plan's at-most-eight bounded row/intention snapshots.
- Each page admits at most 200 operations and five seconds of qualification work; each qualification has a five-second admission timeout. Unsettled work retains its capacity after timeout and cannot install a late grant.
- Page pins release in the Reader finally path, on cancellation/disposal, or after at most 60 seconds. Repeated invalidation checks deduplicate scheduled work.
- The unpinned cache is a 64-entry LRU. Durable per-operation history, rather than cache residency, determines possible later requalification.
- A history row is at most 98 KiB; exact canonical serialized bytes include the row's own counter. Total derived history is capped at 64 MiB including a 1 KiB index reserve, additionally subject to actual storage quota. No space is preallocated and no user material is deleted for capacity.
- A current identity lease remains at most the original 60 seconds; refresh requests fresh read proofs and never changes historical decisions or revives a write capability. Disconnect/unknown/revoke is fail-open. No atomic cross-network revocation is claimed.
- Global deny-only PostgreSQL notification still invalidates every channel for any principal/security update, including unrelated refreshes. This conservative availability/scale limitation requires live multi-account qualification.
