## Workspace composition plan

The frozen architecture permits capturing the route session, safely flushing/leaving, publishing the next route, mounting and restoring anchor/focus. Its no-detach requirement protects live dirty/composing/saving/selected bodies; it does not require clean DOM identity across an intentional, successful route transition. Prefer one internal compose route owned by the existing navigation pipeline over a new hidden-reader parking subsystem.

Before leaving an origin, explicitly reject active composition and unresolved save through its existing owners. Preserve existing body-free query/sort/source-scope/extent/anchor/focus and undo metadata. Re-enter through current authority reads and the same controllers. Standalone Thought return needs its existing entry/history reference; do not retain or restore purged source bodies. Fix ordinary inactive-route admission so queued Topic reads cannot recreate hidden editors after leave. Exact restoration gaps are still being reconciled before implementing this route.

T05 uses the canonical prose workspace width/gutters, heading/subtitle, actual optional Topic selection, literal textarea and Save/Cancel hierarchy. Keep real lazy search/create/retry controls, Copy, conditional explicit relation and truthful status; do not substitute a fixed specimen dropdown or fabricated data.
