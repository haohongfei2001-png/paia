## Honest failure and remaining boundaries

If every durable write fails before a cold owner can claim an earlier clean authorization, that owner blocks subsequent automatic resume, even after storage recovers, and the operation reports unconfirmed failure. An entirely new process cannot infer an intent that no storage recorded and may still see the earlier clean authorization. This physical limit is explicit, not permanent-revocation success.
