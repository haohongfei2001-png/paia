## 3. Adopted product decisions

Remote work converges on AI_MAINTENANCE, AI_ORGANIZE and AI_ASSIST. Capture/UI/search/Smart Filter/Smart Orb/saved-result reads/ordinary external retrieval are provider-free. Free has periodic intelligence and the same durable correctness floor as Pro; Pro changes freshness and interactive volume, not truth quality. All exact allowances and financial envelopes live only in Architecture.

Topic, Section candidates, Context scoped operations and opt-in Pro ambiguous filtering share authorized semantic delta. Identity/evidence admission is independent of scheduler frequency. Info/Rules are stricter than routing. Context manual corrections and removed associations cannot be overwritten or resurrected. The new shared orchestrator submits current-authorized operations through the existing ContextMaintenanceService rather than bypassing its verifier, lineage, revision, association or transaction guards. Adding REMOVE_CANDIDATE/NO_CHANGE semantic operations requires explicit compatible mapping; a body update is not permission to delete protected Items.

AI 整理方式 is a new global all-tier preference: 原话优先 / 平衡整理 (default) / 更加概括. It changes derivative transformation only, not Topic ON/OFF, original bodies, human organization or durable Sections. Selection does not trigger a Library rebuild. Settings receives only one additional selection row.

AI Assist is opt-in intent-driven generation for the current eligible completed reply. It is distinct from ordinary Smart Orb and local Stage 3A. Ordinary external AI retrieval performs authorization/search/evidence/read without another PAIA inference.
