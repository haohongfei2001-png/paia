# PAIA Website v6 — Design Contract

Status: **DESIGN_ONLY / NOT FOR PRODUCTION / OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED**.

This direction returns to the approved visual origin: white spatial field, editorial serif/sans tension, unequal floating input cards, fine relationship paths, quiet material depth, and intentional whitespace. v5's dark tech stage, dashboard-first logic and generic feature-product rhythm are rejected.

## Core idea

The website should not look technological by adding dark surfaces, neon, grids or glass. It should feel like a new AI product because information behaves intelligently in space.

The signature motion is: **statement → fragments arrive → relationships resolve → one deliberate context becomes legible**.

## Non-negotiable visual invariants

- Off-white field; black/graphite type; muted organic accent values. No dark-theme section used as a shortcut to “tech”.
- Display serif remains the emotional anchor; sans is functional metadata only.
- Hero starts as typography and whitespace. Cards are not present at full strength on frame zero.
- Input cards arrive at unequal positions, sizes and depth. Never normalize them into a grid.
- Provider marks use the real vector geometry. Do not redraw or approximate ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini marks.
- Fine paths are structural: they describe the relationship between an input and the assembled context. They are not decorative spaghetti.
- Photography is not required. If a future photo does not carry product meaning and is not visually exceptional, omit it.
- Every micro-label must answer a concrete question: source, time, status, provenance or availability. Decorative slogans are forbidden.
- No fake AI inference, testimonials, film links, accounts, multi-provider support or semantic results.

## Motion grammar

1. **0–18% scroll** — only the hero statement is dominant. The context field is almost absent.
2. **18–48%** — source cards arrive from different directions with very small depth/parallax differences; no bouncing or spring showmanship.
3. **32–68%** — relation paths draw only after both endpoints are perceptible.
4. **52–82%** — the PAIA context object resolves, becomes optically sharp, and holds visual priority.
5. **82–100%** — the field stabilizes. Motion becomes stillness; scattered material has become usable context.

Animations must be reversible under native scroll. No forced scroll, autoplay loop, background particle system or WebGL dependency. Reduced motion shows the resolved composition immediately.

## Homepage rhythm

- Hero: large spatial sequence, approximately 1.9 viewport heights on desktop.
- Three principles: not feature cards. Three pieces of commissioned vector language with one title and one necessary sentence each.
- Product scene: light, layered, product-led. Inputs and prepared context remain simultaneously visible. No generic dashboard metrics.
- Use cases: three concrete use cases only. No decorative microcopy.
- Final scene: abstract architectural spatial form, not lifestyle photography; one statement and one CTA.

## Copy rule

If removing a sentence does not reduce comprehension or trust, remove it.

Allowed copy types: product proposition, concrete capability, boundary, status/provenance, CTA, legal/consent. Everything else requires justification.

## Production gate

No production implementation is authorized by this design branch. Before any v6 production PR, the owner must approve the artboards and motion direction. Implementation must then be screenshot-compared at 1440, 1280 and 390 widths; composition is an acceptance criterion, not a suggestion.
