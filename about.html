A place of your own
for the words you put in.

When you talk to AI, you take time to explain the background, find the words, question an assumption and describe what you have not worked out yet. Those expressions hold the thinking you put in.

After you send them, they can sink into a long conversation history. PAIA starts from your own inputs. It keeps your words and their sources, gives topics a place to continue, and puts useful phrases within reach again.

What they mean next is still yours to decide.
