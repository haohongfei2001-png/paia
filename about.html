About PAIA

A place
for your side of the conversation.

You take time to explain the background, look for answers, question assumptions and describe what you have not worked out yet. Those expressions hold the thinking you put in.

Personal AI
Input Archive

Your words have a life
beyond the conversation.

After you send a message, it may sink into a long conversation history. When you need it again, you may have forgotten where you said it—or that you ever thought that way.

PAIA starts from your own inputs. It keeps your words and their sources, brings expressions together around topics, and puts useful phrases within reach again.

If you want AI to know some of that context, you should control the scope and whether access continues. A personal space begins with the person it belongs to.

Room to remain unfinished

You can still be figuring it out.
You can think differently tomorrow.

A saved expression should not become a final definition of a person. Organization needs to preserve context, conditions and uncertainty, leaving room for a new judgment.

The original story

In the end,
the mailbox remains.

A girl approaches an old mailbox, passes by it, and gradually walks away. We do not know whether she ever posted anything, or what she had meant to express.

What remains holds time, distance and absence. The story does not tell us where the letter went, or supply an ending for her.

PAIA began with this unfinished image. The product gives expressions that have been made a place to remain. What they mean next is still yours to decide.
