WHY PAIA

What you put into a chat
should remain yours.

More of our thinking happens with AI. We explain the background, weigh choices, test assumptions and describe what matters to us. Those expressions already have value, yet they are easily scattered across conversations.

Make those expressions an archive of your own.

PAIA is a personal AI input archive. Input Archive keeps your words and their sources. Thought Library organizes ongoing ideas around your own topics. Personal prompts keep useful phrases close at hand. AI Context lets you decide what a connected AI may learn about your background and material when it needs to.

Organization should help you express yourself.

A passage may be a quotation, an experiment or simply what you thought at that moment. AI organization should remain traceable to its sources and preserve conditions and uncertainty. It must not silently rewrite your words, decide your beliefs or reach beyond your chosen scope.

A letter not yet sent.

The girl seen from behind, the old mailbox and the unsent letter are PAIA’s original image: sending, losing and keeping, and the distance between a person and their own expressions. The product gives those expressions somewhere to remain, and a way to become useful again.
