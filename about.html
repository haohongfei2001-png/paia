A BOUNDARY WE KEEP

Help you organize.
Keep the expression yours.

A passage may be a quotation, an experiment or simply what you thought at that moment. Entering an AI system should not silently turn it into a permanent personal profile.

Organization should remain traceable to its sources and preserve conditions and uncertainty. Your edits, names, order and permission choices keep their own place.
