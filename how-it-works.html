HOW PAIA WORKS

One thought.
More than one moment.

Keep talking with AI. Give the words you have already shared a place to stay, and a way to grow.

Try the interactive example

The complete experience in the approved plan. Currently an invite-only Chrome / ChatGPT beta. View availability

01

Keep the words
you already share.

After installing your invited build, turn on capture. PAIA keeps sent user text from supported ChatGPT pages, together with its source and time.

Pause whenever you need. Importing an official history file is a separate choice.

02

Find the sentence.
Remember the context.

Find inputs by source, conversation or keyword, then read continuously. Revise your working text while keeping the original available.

Filter repeated instructions from reading without deleting the source.

03

Let one question
span many conversations.

Create sections within personal topics and bring related material together. Your names, order, edits and exclusions stay in your hands.

Planned AI organization offers three derived reading styles without replacing the source.

04

Your useful phrases.
Always within reach.

Edit, pin and arrange your personal prompts. In your next AI conversation, choose one to fill the composer and adapt it to the task.

Your existing draft stays. Sending is always your action.

05

When you move on,
let context keep up.

Use four cards for your background, rules, current context and allowed topics. Once live connections are available, authorized AI can read when needed.

Real access starts off. You set the scope and can pause future reads.

Your next task. Not from zero.
