HOW IT WORKS

From your first input
to using it again.

Follow the practical sequence: check capture and source, then edit, organize and reuse. An AI connection is not a prerequisite.

Practice with fictional inputs

The extension requires an invitation. Below is the intended workflow; see availability for current limits. Availability

01 / Check capture02 / Find and edit03 / Organize04 / Reuse exactly05 / Control scope
01

First, check that it is
your input being saved.

After installing an invited desktop Chrome build, enable capture on supported ChatGPT pages. Send a non-critical input, then check its wording, source and time in Input Archive.

Installation does not ingest an entire account history. History import is a separate action. This website captures no chats.

Practice in the complete demo
02

Find the original.
Edit the working version.

Search the archive for “explain” and open the matching input. Edit the working text, then expand its original source. The working version changes, not the historical record of what was sent.

Find within a conversation locates matches without filtering away the rest. An unavailable source is not a verified source.

Practice in the complete demo
03

Give an ongoing question
a topic of its own.

Create a personal topic and arrange related material into your own sections. Open it to read continuously. Manual edits, moves and exclusions do not have to wait for AI organization.

AI organization is a separate derivative reading capability. Its real service is not available, and it cannot override human edits or organization.

Practice in the complete demo
04

Return to the task.
Choose the wording to bring.

Open the board to choose a frequent phrase or search saved inputs. Inspect clipped wording in full before explicitly inserting it. Your current draft stays; you decide when to send.

This illustrates the approved v4 narrow board, still awaiting implementation. Free manual reuse does not depend on Pro AI recommendations.

Practice in the complete demo
05

When context is needed,
choose what to allow.

Maintain information, rules and current context, then choose topics individually. Selecting a card or topic does not silently turn on global access. Closing a parent keeps the lower-level choices.

External AI connections are not available yet. The demo previews scope only; it grants no real permissions and connects no accounts.
