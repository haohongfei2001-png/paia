Product notes/02 · ON EXPRESSION

Not every input
is a belief.

Quotations, experiments and instructions need their own context.

01“What if…”02“Someone said…”03“I think…”

IN THIS NOTE

Explore the example
01

An input can contain several voices.

You might send AI a job description, a draft to rewrite or a hypothetical argument. The record establishes that you sent those words. It does not establish that every sentence is a lasting belief.

Keeping an expression also means keeping its context: where it came from, why it was raised, and what was only a possibility to discuss.

“

What you once said
is not all you believe.

02

Organization must stay traceable.

Original-first, Balanced and More concise change the derived reading view. Each must preserve conditions, perspective and uncertainty, and remain traceable to sources.

AI should not invent a position for you or remove a crucial condition for the sake of a smoother summary. The live AI organization service is still in development.

03

The current choice still belongs to you.

Past words may no longer apply. You can revise working content, manage topics and close AI access.

Let an earlier expression become useful reference material, without making it automatic permission for a future action. A personal archive should keep that boundary clear.
