Keep the phrases that work within reach.

Your way of asking is part of the practice you have built.

You might regularly ask for a counterexample, or prefer the reasoning before the conclusion. These phrases you return to often fit your work better than a generic prompt collection.

Start with expressions you already use.

A useful follow-up can quickly disappear into an old conversation. The next time you need it, you rewrite it from memory. Personal prompts give these expressions a stable place where you can edit, pin and arrange them.

You do not need to learn a prompt taxonomy first. A phrase that already works for you is a worthwhile starting point.

Reuse leaves room to adapt.

Selecting a prompt fills the current composer. Your existing draft stays. You can add this task’s conditions, remove what no longer applies, review it, and then send it.

An old instruction does not become new execution authority just because it appears again. Sending remains the user’s action.

A request and its background have different roles.

Prompt Reuse helps you express what you want AI to do now. AI Context manages the background, rules, current context and topics you allow AI to know. They work together while remaining separately controlled.

The scope of local prompts depends on the invited build and supported pages. Live external Context connections remain in development. The web demo uses sample material and sends nothing to a real AI.
