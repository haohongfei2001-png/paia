How it works

Keep your words.
Build on your thinking.

Start with the AI conversations you already have. Keeping a record should fit into the work you are already doing.

This describes the approved complete experience. The current beta starts with Chrome / ChatGPT; AI organization, real connections and cloud sync are still in development. Current availability

Install, then choose to keep your inputs.

Invited participants follow the setup instructions for the desktop Chrome beta. Once capture is enabled, PAIA keeps sent user text from supported ChatGPT pages. You can pause it. Importing an official history file is a separate action.

ChatGPT“The first release should help creators find material, not generate more.”

Find a sentence, and where it belongs.

Browse sources, projects and conversations, or search for a particular input. Read continuously and revise the working text while keeping the original available. Repeated instructions can be filtered from reading without deleting the source.

Original input“Explore which features could be useful.”
Working version“Validate retrieval first. Leave collaboration out.”

Keep useful phrases within reach.

Edit, pin and arrange useful phrases in your personal prompt library. Next time you use AI, choose one to fill the composer, then send it yourself. No repeated search through old chats, and no automatic sending.

Personal prompt“Give the strongest counterexample and missing variables.”

Keep thinking around your own topics.

Bring related material from different conversations into personal topics and sections. You remain in charge of the structure. Planned AI organization offers Original-first, Balanced and More concise reading views without overwriting your words.

Personal topic / A product’s first stepExploration and trade-offs

Set the scope. Let a connected AI pick it up.

Manage independent items in My Information, My Rules and My Now, and choose allowed topics in My Inputs. Once real connections are available, authorized AI can read when needed. You will not need to assemble a Context packet for each task. Access starts off and can be paused.
