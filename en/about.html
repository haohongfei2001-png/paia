WHY PAIA

What you put into a chat
should outlast that chat.

More of our thinking now happens with AI. We explain the background, weigh a choice, question an assumption, and describe what matters. Over time, these are more than inputs. They are material we have made for ourselves.

PAIA began with a small question: could the expressions we already produce become easier to recover, understand, revise and reuse? Not by asking people to maintain another knowledge base. Not by giving AI access to everything.

Yours to control. Ready for your next AI task.

Input Archive keeps concrete material and its source. Thought Library helps you see a lasting topic across chats. AI Context lets you choose what belongs in the next task. Together they serve one loop: capture, revisit, understand, reuse.

This is an independently developed product in active iteration. Its value must be established through lasting use: easier retrieval, a reason to return, less repetition, and room to think further. Feature counts and passing tests cannot answer those questions.
