Four independent choices

One permission does not grant another.

Keep your inputs

Opt in

Capture sent user text on supported pages. Local reading, search, working-text edits and prompt reuse do not depend on PAIA’s remote model service.

Allow AI organization

Separate permission

Planned AI organization and automatic maintenance use necessary material under processing permission. Permission to process does not grant an external AI open access to your archive.

Allow external AI reading

Off by default

Main access, individual cards and topics each have their own controls. When connections become available, authorized AI can read on demand within that scope.

Sync to your own cloud account

Opt in

Optional sync is controlled separately from AI processing and external reading. Live cloud transfer and new-device recovery remain in development. Sync is not an unlimited backup history.

Clear boundaries
at every level.

Opening a lower scope does not open its parent. Establishing a connection does not grant access.

A closed topic never grants fallback access to the whole Input Archive.

Pausing stops future reads while preserving your scope choices. Withdrawal cannot recall material already delivered to an external AI.

Closing a topic changes that topic’s access. Independent content in other cards keeps its own controls.

A few more distinctions.

How are editing, filtering and deletion different?+

Edits belong to working text while the original source stays separate. Reading filters change visibility; they are not deletion. PAIA and the source platform hold separate copies, so deletion in one does not automatically delete the other. Follow the build’s instructions for removal and permanent deletion.

Does local-first mean data never leaves the device?+

No. Everyday foundations start locally; external processing, reading or sync that you permit has its own data path. Local storage is not itself a promise of encryption or permanent preservation. Keep another source for important material.

Does this website connect to my archive?+

No. Product examples use fictional material and connect to neither personal archives nor a live AI. Demo edits stay in the current page and clear on reload. If you submit the contact form, only its fields are forwarded through FormSubmit.
