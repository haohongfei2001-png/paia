01

Everyday use
starts locally.

Capture, reading, search, working-text edits, manual Context management and local prompt reuse do not depend on PAIA’s remote AI service.

Local-first does not mean material can never leave your device. Planned AI organization and automatic maintenance use necessary material under processing permission. External AI connections have separate reading permissions.

Optional sync is planned through your own browser cloud account. Once enabled, a new device in the same ecosystem can restore successfully synced data. Live transfer and recovery remain in development. There is no automatic cross-ecosystem sync or required PAIA content-cloud account.

02

Make an edit.
Keep the original in view.

The archive retains source records while working text holds your edits. Ordinary editing does not overwrite the original source. Reversible reading filters are neither deletion nor permission.

Thought Library uses personal topics, sections and entries. The three AI organization styles are derived reading views: Original-first, Balanced and More concise. They must preserve sources, conditions and uncertainty, without overriding your names, moves, exclusions or writing.

Connecting an AI does not open access automatically. Once allowed, it may read eligible content on demand, without a Context packet assembled for every task. A closed topic does not grant fallback access to the entire Input Archive.

What happens when I pause or close access?+

Pausing retains your card and topic choices while stopping future reads. Withdrawal cannot recall content already delivered to an external AI.

Closing a topic changes only that topic’s access. Independent items saved in other cards keep their own controls. Opening a lower scope does not open its parent automatically.

04

Every management action
has its own meaning.

Filtering, removing a topic placement, removing an archive record and permanent deletion are different actions. PAIA and the source platform hold separate copies; deletion in one does not automatically delete the other.

Official history-file import, recovery from existing backup files and failure recovery remain in the product plan. Content exports, generating new backups and packaged Context sharing are not part of the current direction. Sync is not an unlimited backup history.

The beta should not be the only copy of important material. Follow the instructions for your invited build and retain the original platform or another independent source. Local storage is not an encryption or permanent lossless-storage guarantee.

05

This website does not
connect to your archive.

There are no analytics scripts, ad trackers or remote fonts. Product examples use current-page memory and clear on reload.

Only submitting the beta application sends form fields through FormSubmit to the project email. Hosting and the form service have their own data-processing boundaries.
