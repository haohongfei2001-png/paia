BETA FOUNDATIONS & TARGET CAPABILITIES

What has a foundation, and what is still being completed.

“Implemented” below means integrated into the development version. It does not imply a store release or availability in your installed build. There is no verified public installer or store download; invitations provide the actual version and setup instructions.

CapabilityCurrent scope
Input ArchiveLocal Chrome / ChatGPT capture, search, working-text editing and source readback are integrated.
Personal promptsLocal reusable phrases, editing, pinning, ordering and manual insertion are implemented. Your invited build determines actual support.
Thought LibraryPersonal topics and section reading have an integrated foundation. The full writing, organization and AI reading experience is still being completed.
Four-card ContextLocal cards and manual content management have an integrated foundation. Full automatic maintenance and real external AI reading are not available.
AI organization and assistanceThree reading styles and explicit user controls are approved. Live model services, quality validation and billing support are not launched.
Sync through your cloud accountThe approach is approved and part of the local foundation exists. Live cloud transport and new-device recovery across the three supported ecosystems are not available.
SOURCES & INSTALLATION

Starting with desktop Chrome and ChatGPT.

Current capture implementation targets supported ChatGPT pages and sent user text. Claude and Gemini in the opening are planned sources. Installation does not automatically collect an entire account history, all AI replies, arbitrary webpages or drafts.

Live sources, devices, signed distribution, updates and import recovery still need validation within their own scope. Follow the instructions for your invited build and retain an independent source for important material.

Capability direction follows the latest approved product contracts. This dated page does not update with every development commit; the instructions accompanying your invitation determine what you can try.
