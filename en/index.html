PRIVATE BY DESIGN. EXPLICIT BY DEFAULT.

More capability.
Clear boundaries.

The closer a product gets to your long-term information, the less “just trust the AI” is enough. Your content, your edits and your permission must remain separate.

01 / LOCAL-FIRST

Local-first storage

Find, edit and prepare context without external AI. Optional external processing is separately authorized. Local storage is not an encryption or loss-proof guarantee.

02 / SOURCE FIDELITY

Your source stays distinct

Ordinary editing does not overwrite source records. Working text and AI organization stay distinct. Today’s edits are not yesterday’s facts.

03 / EXPLICIT CONTROL

Saved is not shared

Select and preview before copy or export. Saving does not automatically grant external AI access. Copies already released cannot be recalled.
