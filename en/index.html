PAIA / FIVE CORE FUNCTIONS

Capture is only the first step.
Four more jobs make it useful again.

The opening already shows job 1: collecting your own inputs across AI products. From there, PAIA helps you review and edit them, reuse prompts, follow ideas by topic, and control personal context.

02

Review and edit your inputs

Treat your inputs as your material, not scattered chat history.

03

05

A personal context center

Let connected AI assistants use only the personal context you authorize.

03 / PROMPT REUSE

After the answer,
your next prompt is close at hand.

After an AI response, PAIA can surface useful next steps and keep your most-used prompts in a lightweight panel. Similar prompts are deduplicated, while the wording and order remain yours to edit.

  • Suggest what you may want to ask next
  • Deduplicate similar frequent prompts
  • Edit, reorder and pin what matters

05 / PERSONAL CONTEXT CENTER

Let AI remember you—
with you in control of what it knows.

PAIA can propose useful personal facts from your own inputs for you to confirm, and you can add facts directly. Each item shows its source, permission scope and usage. AI assistants connected through a plugin or connector request only authorized context for the task, making PAIA a shared personal context and memory center.

  • Suggested facts from inputs + facts you add yourself
  • Every fact has provenance and a current version
  • See what is authorized and what was used
