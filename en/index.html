WHY KEEP IT OVER TIME

Not more to store.
More to build on.

Writing leaves you with arguments. Learning leaves you with explanations. Decisions leave you with conditions you don’t want to rethink. PAIA gives those inputs a source, a working version and your own structure.

Useful material still needs your judgment and maintenance. More records do not guarantee more value, and past wording is not automatically your position today.

CONSIDER IT ALONGSIDE WHAT YOU ALREADY USE

ChatGPT already does a lot.
Where does PAIA fit?

What native features already do

Search finds past chats. Projects bring chats, files and instructions together. Memory can use relevant past information. When those meet your needs, another tool is unnecessary.

Search · Projects · MemoryOfficial OpenAI documentation

PAIA focuses on your inputs themselves

Inspect what you said, keep source and working edits separate, read it across conversations in personal topics, and deliberately reuse exact wording. It does not replace ChatGPT or memory; it lets you work directly with your own material.

Notes and template tools can also save and reuse text. PAIA connects that workflow starting with authorized AI inputs. Whether it earns a place depends on how often you need that path.
