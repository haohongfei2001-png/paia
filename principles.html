DATA & PERMISSIONS

Keep it over time.
Keep it under your control.

Keeping your expressions, asking AI to organize them, allowing an external AI to read them, and syncing to your cloud account are separate choices.

Local and cloudOriginals and editsAI permissionsManagement and recovery

Everyday use starts locally.

Capture, reading, search, working-text edits, manual Context management and local prompt reuse do not depend on PAIA’s remote AI service. This website does not connect to your extension archive.

Local-first does not mean material can never leave your device. Planned AI organization and automatic maintenance will use necessary material under separate processing permission. External AI connections have their own reading permissions. These AI services and connections are not available yet.

Optional sync is planned through your own browser cloud account: Google Drive for Chrome, OneDrive for Edge, and iCloud for Safari. Once enabled, it will let a new device in the same ecosystem restore successfully synced data. Cloud transport and recovery remain in development. Cross-ecosystem sync is not automatic, and PAIA will not require its own content-cloud account.

Revise your working text. Keep the source in view.

The archive retains source records while the working text holds your edits. Ordinary editing does not overwrite the original source. Reading filters are reversible; filtering is neither deletion nor permission for AI.

Thought Library uses your personal topics, sections and entries. AI organization provides a derived reading view: Original-first, Balanced or More concise. It must preserve sources, conditions and uncertainty, and must not override your names, moves, exclusions or writing.

A quotation, hypothesis or third-party passage does not become your lasting belief just because you sent it to AI.

Four cards. Separate controls.

My Information
Background you want AI to know, with independent editing and removal.
My Rules
Your instructions for responses and task boundaries.
My Now
Current projects, recent context and changed circumstances.
My Inputs
Choose which Personal Topics an external AI may read in depth.

The main switch, all four cards and every new topic start closed. Connecting an AI does not open them automatically. Once allowed, an AI may read eligible content when needed; you do not have to assemble and export a packet for every task. Closed topics do not grant fallback access to the whole Input Archive.

Closing a topic changes access to that topic. Content saved independently in other cards keeps its own controls. Pausing or withdrawing permission stops future reads; it cannot recall content already delivered to an external AI.

Clear controls for keeping, editing and recovering.

You control the content in your archive and whether AI may use it. Filtering, removing a topic placement, removing an archive record and permanent deletion are different actions. PAIA and the source platform keep separate copies; deletion in one does not automatically delete the other.

Official history-file import, recovery from existing backup files and failure recovery remain in the product plan. Content exports, generating new backups and packaged Context sharing are no longer part of the current product. Sync is not an unlimited backup history.

The beta should not be the only copy of important material. Keep the original platform or another independent source and follow the instructions for your invited build. Local storage is not an encryption or permanent lossless-storage guarantee.

What does this website do with information?

The website has no analytics scripts, ad trackers, remote fonts or archive connection. Example data stays in the current page and clears on reload. Only submitting the beta application sends form fields through FormSubmit to the project email. Hosting and the form provider have their own data-processing boundaries.
