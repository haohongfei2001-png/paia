The same original words

Starting with a hypothesis

I want to study whether reminders help with focus. Do not turn that into “reminders improve productivity.” For now, it is only a hypothesis.

12 FebFraming the question

Some people find reminders useful; others find them disruptive. Separate timing, task type and the people using them before drawing one conclusion.

03 MarAfter reviewing the literature

The limits of the evidence

More clicks show more responses, not necessarily better focus. I still need an independent measure of task completion.

09 JunInterpreting the results

We only looked at the first few days. These results cannot tell me whether the effect lasts after the novelty wears off.

14 JulWhat remains unanswered

What to test next

Do not put everyone who likes reminders in the treatment group. A difference could reflect their existing habits rather than the reminders.

20 AprPlanning a pilot

Next, observe for longer and compare task types. Keep the possibility that it helps some people at some times; do not claim a universal effect.
