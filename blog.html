A few things worth
getting right about AI context.

A chat history is not task context.

An archive keeps what happened. Context selects what this task needs.

Not every input is a belief.

Quotations, experiments and instructions need their own context.

What belongs in your next prompt?

Enough background. A clear task. Not the entire archive.
