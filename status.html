THE PRODUCT IN PROGRESS

Local test features.
Services still to come.

“Implemented” means an integrated foundation in the development version. It does not imply a public store release or availability in your installed build. There is no verified public installer or store download.

01

Input Archive

LOCAL FOUNDATION

Local Chrome / ChatGPT capture, search, working-text editing and source readback are integrated.

02

Personal prompts

LOCAL FOUNDATION

Reusable phrases, editing, pinning, ordering and manual insertion are implemented. Optional local next-step suggestions start off. The version notes with your invitation explain which features are available.

03

Thought Library

EXPERIENCE IN PROGRESS

Personal topics and section reading have a foundation. Full writing, organization and AI reading are still being completed.

04

Narrow Input Board v4

APPROVED · NOT IMPLEMENTED

The new narrow board design is approved but is not yet implemented in the test extension. You can try its intended interaction here; that does not make it an installable feature today.

05

Four-card Context

LOCAL FOUNDATION

Local cards and manual management have a foundation. Full automatic maintenance and live external AI reading are not available.

06

AI organization & assistance

PLANNED

Three reading styles and user controls are approved. Live model services, quality validation and billing support are not launched.

07

Personal cloud sync

PLANNED

The approach is approved, with part of the local foundation implemented. Live cloud transport and new-device recovery are not available.
